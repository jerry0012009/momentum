本轮 clean replication 口径

  • 样本：Binance 120d / 15m / BTC-USD + ETH-USD + SOL-USD
  • 规则：EMA20/EMA50 cross 为 baseline，保护层只允许加 distance-to-EMA50 threshold 或 retest_hold
  • trade on：出现 EMA cross，且满足对应保护层
  • trade off：不满足保护层则 no-trade
  • 执行：next-bar open | 1 ATR stop | 2 ATR target | 8-bar time stop | 6bps/side
  • spec：reports/artifacts/scout_ema_shielding_15m/clean_room_spec_v1.csv
